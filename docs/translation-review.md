# What waits for a native reader

Nothing, for now: the site is English only (docs/decisions.md, 8). The Finnish and Swedish pages and the site's own words in those languages, all written by a machine and read by nobody, went out with the languages and are in the history at 424c56f. When a language comes back, every text in it says `machine: true` or names its native reader, as the site's rules say, and this file lists what waits.

## Not to be translated by a machine

The bylaws, the terms of use and the privacy policy. They exist in the languages people wrote them in, and are read in those: the bylaws in Finnish, under the English pages.
